Select your professional profile to access tailored guides and documentation:

:::note[👤 1. User Guide]
For prompt creators, business analysts, and operational team members.
* **[Creating & Managing Prompts](/user-guide/creating-prompts/)**: Markdown editing, token estimation, and the "Improve" assistant.
* **[Library, Categories & Tags](/user-guide/categories-and-library/)**: Organise, filter, and move prompt assets (4-tag rule).
* **[Templates & Dynamic Variables](/user-guide/templates-and-variables/)**: Build reusable templates with typed variables and previews.
* **[Prompt Template Catalogue](/user-guide/template-catalog/)**: Explore and instantiate 4,000 production-ready templates.
* **[Versioning & Diff Tool](/user-guide/versioning-and-history/)**: Immutable change history, visual diff comparisons, and safe rollbacks.
* **[Prompt Lab: Architect & Improver](/user-guide/prompt-lab/)**: Advanced prompt engineering studio, iterative generation, and prompt hardening.
* **[Testing & Multi-Model Evaluation](/user-guide/testing-and-evaluation/)**: Test in real time and benchmark models (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google).
* **[Collaboration & Comments](/user-guide/collaboration-and-comments/)**: Team discussions, peer mentions, and review tracking.
* **[Browser Extension](/user-guide/browser-extension/)**: Fast access to favourites and direct insertion into web applications.
:::

:::note[👥 2. Manager Guide]
For department heads and team managers.
* **[Organisation & Team Hierarchy](/manager-guide/organization-and-teams/)**: Structure organisations and manage team member onboarding.
* **[Workspace Management](/manager-guide/workspaces-management/)**: Provision workspaces, isolate sensitive assets, and assign access tiers.
* **[AI Integrations & Team Keys](/manager-guide/team-api-keys/)**: Connect foundational providers and govern shared team credentials.
* **[Analytics, Costs & Alerts](/manager-guide/cost-tracking/)**: Monitor token consumption, track expenditure, and configure budget alerts.

* **[System API Keys & Infrastructure](/admin-guide/system-api-keys/)**: Power native features (Improve, Prompt Lab) and monitor rate limits.
* **[Authorised AI Models Governance](/admin-guide/authorized-ai-models/)**: Enforce model allowlists, block shadow AI, and filter providers.
* **[RBAC Roles & Permissions Matrix](/admin-guide/rbac-matrix/)**: Configure detailed permission tiers across all operational scopes.
* **[Security, Audit & Compliance](/admin-guide/security-compliance/)**: AES-256 encryption, tamper-evident audit trails, and GDPR / SOC 2 controls.

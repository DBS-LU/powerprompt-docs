Power Prompt enforces robust authentication controls to safeguard accounts, proprietary prompt assets, and connected AI model credentials.

---

## Signing In to Power Prompt

1. Navigate to the sign-in URL of your Power Prompt instance.
2. Enter your registered corporate email address.
3. Provide your password and click **Sign In**.
4. If Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) is active on your profile or mandated by your organisation, enter the 6-digit one-time code generated by your authenticator app.

---

## Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)

Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) provides a critical defense against credential compromise:
* **Organisation-Wide Enforcement:** Enterprise organisations can mandate MFA for all active members as part of their compliance baseline.
* **Role-Based Enforcement:** Elevated roles (*Platform Administrators*, *Organisation Managers*, *Team Managers*) must maintain active MFA.
* **User-Level Activation:** Any individual user can independently enable MFA from their personal profile settings.

Power Prompt supports industry-standard TOTP authenticator applications, including Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, 1Password, and Bitwarden.

---

## Password Recovery

If you lose or forget your password:
1. On the sign-in screen, click **Forgot password?**.
2. Enter your registered email address.
3. Open the password reset email delivered to your inbox.
4. Click the secure, time-limited single-use link.
5. Enter a new, compliant password and submit.

---

## Session Timeouts and Account Lockout

To protect enterprise data across shared or unattended workstations:

- **Inactivity Session Expiration:** Following a prolonged period of inactivity, the session expires and requires re-authentication.
- **Brute-Force Protection:** Accounts are temporarily locked after multiple consecutive failed authentication attempts.
- **Complexity Policies:** Passwords must meet organisational standards regarding character length, diversity, and absence from compromised credential registries.

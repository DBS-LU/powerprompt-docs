**Power Prompt** is an enterprise platform dedicated to the adoption, governance, and industrialisation of generative artificial intelligence.

It enables users, teams, and organisations to centralise their AI knowledge, manage and improve their prompts, standardise best practices, govern usage, and deploy AI consistently and securely at scale.

The platform provides a unified environment to design, organise, test, share, and track the evolution of enterprise AI assets, whilst integrating multiple model providers and ensuring full traceability of changes and usage.

Power Prompt addresses a major challenge encountered by numerous organisations: transforming often scattered, individual, and unmanaged AI initiatives into a structured, collaborative, measurable, and reusable corporate asset.

Beyond prompt management, Power Prompt helps organisations capitalise on their expertise, accelerate AI adoption, control operational costs, and progressively build sustainable AI-driven processes, workflows, and capabilities.

## Subscription Plans and Target Audiences

Power Prompt adapts to different operational scales across three subscription tiers:

| Subscription Tier | Target Audience | Initial Setup |
|---|---|---|
| **Personal Account** (Free or Paid) | Individual professional | Personal workspace and default group created automatically |
| **Team Plan** | Department or team with multiple users | Initial setup required by a Team Manager |
| **Enterprise Plan** | Entire organisation with advanced governance | Initial onboarding executed by an Organisation Manager |

Available features vary depending on subscription level, assigned role permissions, and organisation-wide policies.

## Key Platform Concepts

Understanding the relationship between these five core entities ensures optimal use of Power Prompt:

### 1. Organisation
An **organisation** represents a company, corporate entity, or client tenant within Power Prompt. It encompasses all teams, workspaces, and users within that tenant.
* *Example:* Organisation: `ACME Consulting`

### 2. Team
A **team** gathers members working on a shared operational scope (business department, functional unit, cross-functional project, or AI Centre of Excellence). Each team can be linked to one or more workspaces.
* *Examples:* Teams: `Marketing`, `Product`, `Customer Support`, `Human Resources`.

### 3. Workspace

A **workspace** is the collaborative environment in which users and teams create, organise, test, share, and govern their AI assets.

Each workspace forms an independent collaboration perimeter and specifically contains:

- Prompt libraries.
- Classification categories.
- Members, roles, and access permissions.
- Telemetry dashboards and usage metrics.

A workspace enables the structuring of team or organisational AI knowledge whilst ensuring security, collaboration, and operational activity traceability.

### 4. Prompt

A **prompt** is an instruction or set of structured directives designed to be executed by a generative artificial intelligence model.

Within Power Prompt, a prompt can specifically include:

- A title and description.
- Content authored in Markdown.
- Dynamic input variables and parameters.
- Up to four classification tags.
- A business or functional category.
- Contextual comments and collaborative discussion threads.
- A comprehensive history of versions and modifications.
- One or more recommended AI models.
- Execution parameters (temperature, maximum response length, etc.).
- Utilisation and performance metrics.
- User evaluations and feedback ratings.

Each prompt constitutes a reusable, maintainable, and governed AI asset that can be shared, improved, and leveraged across the organisation.

### 5. Prompt Typology and Templates

Power Prompt distinguishes two primary types of prompts:

#### Simple Prompt

A **simple prompt** is composed exclusively of static content ready for immediate execution, requiring no additional input.

Simple prompts are particularly suited to one-off requests, direct instructions, and recurring queries without dynamic parameters.

#### Parameterized Prompt (Prompt Template)

A **parameterized prompt** (or **prompt template**) contains a fixed structure and one or more dynamic variables enclosed in double curly braces `{{...}}`.

Example:

```text
Draft meeting minutes for {{audience}} with an emphasis on {{objective}}.
```

Upon execution, Power Prompt automatically generates an interactive input form prompting the user to supply values for each variable, tailoring the prompt without modifying the underlying reference template.

## The Prompt Lifecycle in Power Prompt

Power Prompt supports the full prompt lifecycle, from initial design through to large-scale adoption across the organisation.

1. **Creation & Enhancement:** Author your prompts in Markdown, estimate their token consumption, and enhance their quality using built-in assistance and optimisation features.
2. **Structuring & Organisation:** Organise your prompts into thematic categories, apply classification tags, and build searchable, easily maintainable AI knowledge libraries.
3. **Standardisation & Reusability:** Transform your prompts into reusable templates using dynamic variables and standardise best practices across teams and business functions.
4. **Testing & Validation:** Execute your prompts across different artificial intelligence models, compare the resulting outputs, and identify the highest-performing configurations for each use case.
5. **Version Management & Traceability:** Track the complete evolution of your prompts with version history, compare modifications, and restore any previous version at any time.
6. **Collaboration & Governance:** Share knowledge, discuss via comments, collect user feedback, and disseminate organisation-approved prompts.
7. **Operational Adoption:** Rapidly access your prompts from personal libraries or the browser extension to leverage them daily in your professional workflows.
8. **Analytics & Continuous Improvement:** Measure prompt utilisation, identify top-performing assets, and progressively capitalise on your organisation's best practices.
